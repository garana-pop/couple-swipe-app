Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Kotaeai account and all associated data anytime using the methods below.

What Gets Deleted

  • Deleted Immediately: Authentication credentials, profile (display name, locale, push token), all of your quiz answers, pair membership, and—if you are the last member of a pair—the pair record, daily question sets, and AI reports.
  • Subscriptions: If you have an active Couple Premium subscription, please cancel auto-renewal separately in Google Play Subscriptions or Apple App Store Subscriptions.

Option 1: Delete In-App (Recommended — Works for Anonymous Accounts Too)

  1. Open the Kotaeai app.
  2. Tap the Menu (≡) icon in the top-right corner of the Home screen.
  3. Select "Delete Account" and confirm to permanently erase your data immediately.

Option 2: Delete via Web (For Google-Linked Accounts)

Even if you already uninstalled the app, you can sign in with your linked Google account below to delete your data immediately.

Option 3: Uninstalled Without Google Linking

Anonymous accounts store no personally identifiable information (such as email addresses). Any inactive pair data is automatically and permanently deleted after 180 days of inactivity.
