Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026 · App: Kotaeai

This Privacy Policy explains how Kotaeai ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Identifiers: An automatically generated user ID via anonymous authentication. Only if you choose to link a Google account for backup/device transfer do we collect your Google email address and authentication identifier.
  • User-Provided Data: Your display name (nickname), pair invite code, daily quiz swipe selections (Up/Down/Left/Right), and device language/time zone settings used to localize notifications and daily resets.
  • Push Notification Tokens: An Expo Push Token to deliver partner activity notifications (only when you grant notification permission).
  • Subscription Status: Active status, expiration date, and product identifier for Couple Premium. Payment card details are processed exclusively by Google Play or Apple App Store and are never stored by the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • Synchronizing daily questions between paired partners, revealing results once both complete them, and showing past answer comparisons.
  • Delivering localized push notifications when your partner finishes answering.
  • Generating AI compatibility and reflection reports exclusively for subscribed Couple Premium pairs.
  • Supporting device transfers and preventing abuse (rate limiting on invite codes).

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted infrastructure providers to operate the App. We never sell user data or share it for advertising purposes.

  • Supabase (Database, authentication, and server functions)
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) / APNs (Push notification delivery)
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store / RevenueCat (Subscription billing and verification)
  • Google Gemini API (Only when a Couple Premium user requests an AI report are answer-history texts sent for analysis. Free users' data is never sent to AI APIs.)

4. Data Retention & Automatic Deletion

  • Inactive pairs (no activity for 180+ days) are automatically deleted from our database.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data immediately from within the App or via the web. See the Account & Data Deletion Guide.

5. Security

All network traffic is encrypted via HTTPS (TLS). Our database enforces strict Row Level Security (RLS) so that only members of a pair can access their pair data, and answers remain hidden from your partner until both complete the day's questions.
